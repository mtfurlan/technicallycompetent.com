This is a summary of [Brian Krebs](https://krebsonsecurity.com/)'s series on
credit reporting agencies and other important accounts

* [Krebs on Security: Why & Where You Should Plant Your Flag](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2020/08/why-where-you-should-you-plant-your-flag/)
* [Krebs on Security: Credit Freezes are Free: Let the Ice Age Begin](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2018/09/credit-freezes-are-free-let-the-ice-age-begin/)
* [Krebs on Security: Think You’ve Got Your Credit Freezes Covered? Think Again.](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2018/05/another-credit-freeze-target-nctue-com/)
* [Krebs on Security: How to Opt Out of Equifax Revealing Your Salary History](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2017/11/how-to-opt-out-of-equifax-revealing-your-salary-history/)
* [consumerfinace.gov: List of consumer reporting companies][consumer reporting companies]


You also should log into all the stupid accounts every year or so, because they
change things and break freezes or just delete your account.

Never give any of them money for anything, it's all free.

## Places you should place a freeze / opt out
### Misc
* [ChexSystems](https://www.chexsystems.com/)
  * checked when opening bank accounts
* [NCTUE](https://nctueconsumerportal.com/)
  * National Consumer Telecommunications and Utilities Exchange
  * checked for opening phone accounts
  * owed by equifax
* [opt out of pre-approved offers](https://www.optoutprescreen.com/)
  * > ID thieves like to intercept offers of new credit and insurance sent via postal mail, so it’s a good idea to opt out of pre-approved credit offers.
* [ lexis nexis / sagestream](https://consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com/freeze)
  * checked for a bunch of different stuff
